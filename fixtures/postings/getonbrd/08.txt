[projects]
PaperStreet is looking for a full-time or part-time developer to help us build, improve, and maintain internal AI tools, automations, integrations, and reporting systems.
This is a practical development role focused on using existing AI platforms and APIs to solve business problems. We are not looking for someone to build or train large language models from scratch.
The right person will be comfortable connecting systems, working with APIs, processing data, building simple dashboards, troubleshooting integrations, and helping turn internal ideas into reliable tools.
In short, we need someone who can also assist with other development, automation, reporting, and technical projects. If hired part-time, the role can increase to full-time.
