[projects]
Niuro connects elite LATAM engineers with top-tier U.S. companies, handling all administrative tasks so engineers can focus on high-impact work. For this role, you'll join a fast-growing U.S. startup that builds an operating platform for self-storage operators. As it lands larger enterprise accounts, it needs its first Forward Deployed Engineer: the technical bridge between the platform and each customer's operation.Y ou'll work directly with the two founders and with customer leadership teams, both remotely and during scheduled customer-site visits in the U.S.

[functions]
As a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, you will lead the technical integration of the platform directly into customer environments. You will:
- Lead integration projects inside customer environments, connecting the platform to the systems the operator already runs.
- Map customer operational workflows and translate them into platform configurations, data models and automated integrations.
- Build and maintain production agentic AI systems using Model Context Protocol (MCP) to manage tools, context and memory.
- Own onboarding acceleration: configuration, data migration and ingestion pipelines.
- Guide deployments through customer IT and cybersecurity reviews.
- Serve as the named technical contact for assigned accounts.
- Identify repeatable integration patterns and turn them into productization candidates for the core engineering team.
This is a remote role based in LATAM with required travel: expect periodic trips to customer sites in the U.S. of up to two weeks at a time, sometimes as the sole company representative on site.
